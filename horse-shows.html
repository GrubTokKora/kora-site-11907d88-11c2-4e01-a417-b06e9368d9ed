Good to know

Questions competitors ask

How do I enter a Marchant Farm horse show?

Entries and show information are handled online at horseshowsonline.com. The show office is run by Ruth Nicodemus of Serenity Show Stable, reachable on 914-755-7562.

What time do classes start?

Classes begin at 8:30am.

What divisions are offered?

Hunters, jumpers, equitation, derbies, classics and leadline, under CHJA, NEHC, Marshall and Sterling and FW-PHA.

Where do trailers enter?

Horse trailers use the show entrance at 12 Marchant Road, Redding, Connecticut, which is separate from the main farm drive on Chestnut Woods Road.

Is there an indoor ring if the weather is bad?

Yes. There is an indoor ring, an outdoor ring and a dedicated warm-up ring.

Ride with us this season

Entries are online. If you are new to the series and want to school here first, ask about a lesson or a hire of the rings.
