Boarding & Leasing

Horse Boarding and Leasing in Redding, CT

Large open-air box stalls with a camera and a fan in every one, more than 27 paddocks, and staff who know your horse. Plus a lease programme for riders not ready to buy.

Ask about a stall

Boarding

A gorgeous, brand new facility — and staff who actually care.

Our horse boarding facilities are top-notch: spacious stalls, ample turnout space, cameras and fans in each stall, and a caring staff. Indoor arena, outdoor arena, lunging pad, jump field and loads of trails to enjoy.

What is on site

Indoor arena
100 × 200 feet, with ample heated seating and a viewing booth

Cross-country field

The daily reality

What boarding here looks like on a Tuesday

The parts of boarding that matter are not the ones in the brochure photograph.

Stalls cleaned daily

13 by 15 foot open-air box stalls, mucked every day, with grooming and wash stalls and tack rooms alongside.

Eyes on your horse

A camera in every stall, and a fan in every stall for the summer.

Turnout that works in mud season

More than 27 paddocks, a mix of all-weather footing and grass, so turnout does not stop when the weather does.

Somewhere to ride, always

A heated-seating indoor arena, an all-weather sand ring, a lunging pad and a jump field.

Trails from the yard

Miles of trails across the property, so hacking out does not mean trailering.

A show series at home

Rated shows run on the property, so your first outing of the season can be a walk from your own stall.

Leasing

Ownership, without the ownership

Major Motion offers a lease programme that replicates owning a horse, which allows you to show and ride at your leisure.

It suits the rider who has outgrown a weekly lesson but is not ready to buy, and the family who wants to know what ownership actually asks of them before committing to it. Leases are matched to the rider, so the conversation starts with where you are and what you want to do this season.

Good to know

Questions owners ask

What size are the stalls?

Large 13 by 15 foot open-air box stalls, cleaned daily, each with a camera and a fan.

How much turnout is there?

More than 27 paddocks, a mix of all-weather footing and grass, so horses go out through mud season as well as summer.

Is there an indoor arena for winter?

Yes, 100 by 200 feet, with ample heated seating and a viewing booth.

Can I bring my own trainer?

Ask. Trainers relocating a programme should look at the Train at Marchant Farm page, which covers the facility from a trainer's point of view.

What is included in a horse lease?

Leases replicate ownership so that you can show and ride at your leisure. The exact terms depend on the horse and the rider, so they are agreed case by case.

Come and see the barn

Stalls, turnout, arenas and trails — walk it before you move a horse. Visits are by appointment.
