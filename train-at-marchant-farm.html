For Trainers

Train at Marchant Farm

A purpose-built hunter/jumper facility in Fairfield County with room for another programme — indoor and outdoor arenas, a jump field, trails and 27-plus paddocks.

Talk about relocating

For independent trainers

Bring your programme. The facility is already built.

If you are running a programme out of a barn that is too small, too far from your clients, or short of winter footing, this page is the specification sheet you actually want to see.

Marchant Farm sits on more than 50 acres in Redding, in the middle of Fairfield County's client base, twenty minutes from Ridgefield and Danbury and inside an hour of Westchester. There is a rated show series already running on the property.

Good to know

Questions trainers ask

Is Marchant Farm accepting new trainers?

We are open to conversations with independent trainers looking to relocate or expand a programme. Availability and terms are agreed case by case.

How many stalls are available?

Stall availability changes through the season. Send an enquiry and you will get the current position.

What is the footing like?

The sand ring and cross-country field have brand new top-of-the-line all-weather footing and drainage. The indoor arena is 100 by 200 feet with heated seating and a viewing booth.

Is there a show series on site?

Yes. Marchant Farm runs a recognised series under CHJA, NEHC, Marshall and Sterling and FW-PHA, with an indoor ring, an outdoor ring and a dedicated warm-up ring.

Come and walk the facility

Bring a client if you like. The arenas, the stalls, the turnout and the trails take about an hour to see properly.
