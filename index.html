Our Story

A True Connecticut
Gem Since 1750

Marchant Farm, LLC, is a family-owned equestrian center and farm nestled in the rolling hills of Redding, Connecticut. With a history dating back to 1750, we've spent generations nurturing this land — and the community around it.

We offer a wide range of products, including seasonal fruits, vegetables, honey, and eggs, as well as firewood for those cozy winter nights. Our equestrian services include horseback riding lessons, horse boarding, and horse leases — all delivered with the care and expertise of people who truly love this land and its animals.

At Marchant Farm, we want to give our customers the freshest and best products and the finest equestrian experience possible. We welcome you like a neighbor — because to us, that's exactly what you are.
