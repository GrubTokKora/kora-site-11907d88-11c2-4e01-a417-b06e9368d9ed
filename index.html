Redding, Connecticut · Established 1750

One property, four very different reasons to come.

Marchant Farm is a working farm and equestrian centre built around a fieldstone house that has stood since 1750. It is also, for a handful of dates each year, a private estate you can have entirely to yourself.

Most venues are one thing. This one is pasture and jump field, kitchen garden and beehives, a 100 × 200 indoor arena, miles of trails, a farm stand and a guest cottage — inside a single gate, ninety minutes from Manhattan.

Equestrian

A show barn that happens to sit on a farm

Major Motion Farm runs the riding program on site: lessons from first sitting trot through hunter/jumper, boarding, and a lease program for riders not ready to buy. Serenity Show Stable trains and runs the rated show series here too.

The facility behind both: a 100 × 200 indoor arena with heated seating, an all-weather sand ring and cross-country field, a lunging pad, a jump field, 13 × 15 open-air box stalls with cameras and fans, more than 27 paddocks, and miles of trails.
