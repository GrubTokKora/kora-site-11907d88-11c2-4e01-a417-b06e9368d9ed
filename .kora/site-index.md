## support files
Files that are not pages. A line marked [content] holds words or data a visitor reads, so a
change to the site's content can land there; the rest only make the site work or look right.
- `llms.txt` — The site's main business details, contact information, page list, and brand colors for LLMs and site description: Marchant Farm, Weddings, Corporate Retreats, Private Events, Nonprofit Fundraisers, Photo & Film, Riding Lessons, Boarding, Horse Shows, Farmers Market, Farm Products, Events Calendar, Stay, About, Reviews, Contact, Blog  [content]
- `robots.txt` — 26 bytes — too small to hold content
- `sitemap.xml` — 110 bytes — too small to hold content
- `assets/site.css` — Stylesheet defining global CSS variables, typography, layout, and UI component styles for the Marchant Farm website.
- `js/main.js` — Marchant Farm horse show dates, weekly farm stand inventory, prices, and notes: Raw wildflower honey, Free-range eggs, Seasonal vegetables, Seasonal fruit, Firewood by the bag, Firewood by the pallet  [content]

## shared (every page)
The header, navigation, mobile menu and footer are propagated from index.html to every other page by
`shell_propagation`. A change to any of them is made on index.html alone and copied automatically.
