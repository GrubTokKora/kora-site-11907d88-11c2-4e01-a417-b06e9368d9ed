Events & Venue Rentals

A Private Farm Estate for Unforgettable Gatherings

Set on more than 50 scenic acres in Redding, Connecticut, Marchant Farm offers a unique backdrop for weddings, corporate retreats, celebrations, equestrian events and private gatherings.

Start an enquiryTrusted partners

Why here

Most venues give you a room. This one gives you the whole farm.

Marchant Farm has been a working farm since 1750. It is still one — there are hens, hives, a kitchen garden and eighty-odd horses' worth of infrastructure on it. That is exactly why it works for events: nothing here was built to look like a venue, so nothing here looks like everyone else's.

When you book a date, you are not booking a ballroom inside a bigger operation. You are booking a private estate with a gate, a drive, parking, and no other party happening on the other side of a partition wall.

The settings

Six places to put an event

Most events use two or three of these together — ceremony in one, dinner in another, drinks somewhere in between.

The stone house and terrace

The oldest thing on the property and the one everybody photographs. Fieldstone walls, two chimneys, evergreens behind, a dry-stone wall along the front. Ceremonies, portraits, drinks receptions.

Open pasture and paddocks

Level, fenced, private and large. This is where a tent goes, where a marquee dinner sits, where a band sets up without a neighbour in earshot.

The kitchen garden

Raised beds, timber trellises, a gate with the tools still hanging on it. Intimate gatherings, farm-to-table dinners, rehearsal suppers and shoots.

The arenas

A 100 × 200 indoor arena with heated seating, and a 150 × 270 all-weather sand ring. Covered capacity when the weather turns, and the setting for equestrian events and demonstrations.

The orchard and meadow

Long light, young trees, nothing in the frame but the tree line. Ceremonies, portraits, lawn games and the quiet corner of a bigger event.

Trails and woodland

Miles of trails through 50-plus acres. Guided walks, trail rides, a proper arrival down a gravel drive, and somewhere for a retreat to go and think.

Good to know

Questions planners ask first

How many guests can Marchant Farm hold?

It depends on which settings you combine and how you use them — a seated dinner under a tent on open pasture and a cocktail reception on the stone-house terrace have very different limits. Send your guest count with your enquiry and we will confirm exactly what works for that number.

Do you have a rain plan?

Yes. The 100 by 200 foot indoor arena has heated seating and a viewing booth, and it is the standing weather contingency for events held on the grounds.

Can we bring our own caterer and vendors?

Yes. The vendor list is open. We also keep a Trusted Partners directory of caterers, florists, tent companies, photographers and planners who have already worked here and know the access, the power and the ground.

Is there parking?

There is parking on the property, and a separate trailer entrance used for horse trailers and large deliveries, which keeps tent and catering trucks off your guests' arrival route.

Can guests stay on the farm?

There is a private guest cottage on the estate, booked through Airbnb. It is popular with wedding parties, retreat organisers and families in for horse-show weekends.

How far is Marchant Farm from New York City?

Marchant Farm is at 55 Chestnut Woods Road in Redding, Connecticut, in Fairfield County, roughly ninety minutes from Manhattan and well inside a comfortable drive from Westchester, New Haven and Hartford.
