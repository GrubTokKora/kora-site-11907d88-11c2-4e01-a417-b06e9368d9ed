Major Motion Farm at Marchant Farm

Schedule a riding evaluation

The programme

A show barn, on a farm, twenty minutes from Ridgefield.

Major Motion Farm runs the riding programme at Marchant Farm: lessons, boarding and a lease programme that lets a rider show and ride at their leisure without buying a horse.

What sits behind it is not a converted field. It is a 100 × 200 indoor arena with heated seating, an all-weather sand ring and cross-country field, a jump field, a lunging pad, more than 27 paddocks, and miles of trails across the property.

Showing

A rated show series in your own back field

Marchant Farm runs its own recognised horse show series — CHJA, NEHC, Marshall & Sterling and FW-PHA — with hunters, jumpers, equitation, derbies, classics and leadline. Riders based here school on the same footing they compete on.

Serenity Show Stable, the show programme run by Ruth Nicodemus, is also based at the farm and manages the series.

Good to know

Questions new riders ask

Do you teach complete beginners?

Yes. Lessons start with first-time riders and run through to riders competing in the hunter/jumper ring, so the programme does not run out of room as a rider improves.

What is a riding evaluation?

A first session where an instructor watches you or your child ride, or walks a first-timer through the basics, so lessons start at the right level rather than a guessed one. It is how every new rider begins here.

Do I need my own horse?

No. Lessons are taught on the farm's horses, and the lease programme is designed for riders who want the experience of ownership without buying.

Where is Major Motion Farm?

At Marchant Farm, 55 Chestnut Woods Road, Redding, Connecticut, in Fairfield County — about twenty minutes from Ridgefield and Danbury.
