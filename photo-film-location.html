Photo & Video Shoots

A Photo and Film Location in Redding, Connecticut

Fieldstone, timber, pasture, orchard, arenas and horses — fifteen distinct looks inside one gate, ninety minutes from New York.

For producers and photographers

One location, fifteen setups, no travel between them.

A 1750 fieldstone house. A dry-stone wall. Cedar shingle and stacked firewood. A timber garden gate. Raised beds and trellises. Beehives. Chicken coops. Open pasture. A young orchard. Post-and-rail fencing. A 100 × 200 indoor arena. A sand ring. Miles of wooded trail. And horses.

What shoots here

Brand shoots
Rural, heritage, food, outdoor and equestrian brands
Fashion & editorial
Stone and timber exteriors, arena interiors, pasture at golden hour
Engagement sessions
The orchard, the drive and the stone house, without a permit queue
Commercial filming
Exclusive use, vehicle access and a separate trailer entrance
Equestrian content
Horses, tack, stalls, arena and trails, with staff on site
Product & still life
Honey, eggs, produce and firewood, photographed where they come from

The looks

What the location actually gives you

Shot on ordinary days, unstyled. Assume better light and a crew.

Good to know

Questions producers ask first

Can Marchant Farm be booked as a photography location?

Yes. The property is available for brand, fashion, editorial, engagement and commercial shoots, either alongside normal farm activity or on an exclusive-use basis.

Are horses available for shoots?

Horses and handlers can be arranged with the riding staff. Say so in your brief, because it affects scheduling and the quote.

Is there vehicle access for a production truck?

There is a gravel drive and a separate trailer entrance used for horse trailers and large deliveries, which suits grip trucks and equipment trailers.

Do you allow drone filming?

Aerial work has been shot here before. Confirm it in your brief along with your operator's credentials and we will approve it with the date.

Send a shoot brief

Dates, crew size, whether you need horses and whether you need the property to yourself. That is enough for a quote.
